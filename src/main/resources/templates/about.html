What to know about the world-famous FIFA tournament.

The 23rd FIFA World Cup heads to North America this Summer, where Argentina will look to defend their 2022 title win during the last World Cup in Qatar. Canadians have a lot to be excited about, as their country will host the World Cup for the first time, with select matches being played at BMO Field (known as Toronto Stadium for the duration of the tournament) in Toronto, and BC Place in Vancouver. Besides Canada, matches are set to be played in the United States and Mexico, culminating in the final at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey.

For fans of football heritage, these games will be the final chance for many of the sport's greatest athletes to add another accolade to their storied resumes, including players like Ronaldo, Messi, Neymar, and Salah.
