About the Tournament

The 2026 FIFA World Cup is a landmark edition of the world's most-watched sporting competition, co-hosted across three nations: the United States, Canada, and Mexico.

For the first time in history, the tournament will feature 48 national teams competing across 16 venues, making it the largest World Cup ever held. Matches will take place in iconic cities including New York, Los Angeles, Toronto, Mexico City, and more — spanning two continents and uniting fans from every corner of the globe.

Canada is proud to be co-hosting its first-ever FIFA World Cup, with BC Place in Vancouver and BMO Field in Toronto among the selected venues. This edition represents a celebration of football in North America and a once-in-a-generation opportunity for Canadian fans to experience the world's game at home.
