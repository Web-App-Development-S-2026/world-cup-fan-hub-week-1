⚽ World Cup Fan & Event Hub

The 2026 FIFA World Cup sets several historic milestones. For the first time, the tournament has expanded from the traditional 32-team format to include 48 national teams. Teams are divided into 12 groups of four with 104 total matches, up from 64 in 2022. This is the first FIFA World Cup jointly hosted by three countries: the United States, Mexico, and Canada. Matches are taking place across 16 world-class host cities.
